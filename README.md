# Beyond the Big Clubs: Football Performance Analytics, Data-Driven Recruitment and Financial Value in the Premier League

Portfolio project analyzing Brentford FC, Brighton & Hove Albion and AFC Bournemouth
(the "Big 3B's") across three Premier League seasons (2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25).

## Status: In progress

## Investigation A — Match Performance vs Title Contenders
Exploring how these three clubs performed against Premier League title contenders
(defined as each season's final top-4 finishers), and whether any results affected
the outcome of the title race.

**Key early finding:** In 2023-24 — the tightest title race of the three seasons
(2-point gap between champions Man City and runners-up Arsenal) — none of the 3B's
took a single point off either club across all 12 head-to-head meetings.
By contrast, in the less tightly contested 2022-23 and 2024-25 seasons, all three
clubs recorded at least one win against the eventual champion or runner-up
(e.g. Brentford's double over Man City in 2022-23).
